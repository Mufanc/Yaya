Example:
  yaya frame get 0x0 -t 0x12 --registers 0:int,2:object

Copy fresh frame and thread IDs from thread stack. Specify the type of each slot; yaya does not infer it. Use decimal JDWP slot numbers without prefixes (0..2147483647), e.g. 0:int,2:object. Types: boolean, byte, char, short, int, long, float, double, object. Requires a suspended thread. Optimized-out or unavailable slots may be rejected by the VM.
