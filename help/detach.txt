Example:
  yaya detach

Uses JDWP VirtualMachine.Dispose, not VM.Exit. Ends the current connection; daemon mode also removes its socket and session directory. The session ID and all its runtime references must no longer be used.
Requires a compatible business IPC version. To end a daemon after a version mismatch, use 'yaya daemon stop'; its shutdown attempts Dispose but does not report Dispose success.
